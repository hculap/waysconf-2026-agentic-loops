# 00 — Orient

You have just been pointed at a repository you have never seen. Before anything else, work out what it is.

Read these, in this order, and read them properly rather than skimming for keywords:

1. `README.md`
2. `docs/CANON.md`
3. `brief/BRIEF.md`
4. `brief/ACCEPTANCE.md`
5. `AGENTS.md`
6. `loop/PROGRESS.md`

Then answer, in under 400 words total, with a file path beside every claim:

1. **What is being built.** Five sentences at most. What the thing is, who it is for, and what it is not.
2. **What "done" means.** One sentence, naming the exact command that decides it and what it returns.
3. **The sections.** List them in canonical order, by name, with nothing else attached.
4. **The rules you are under.** The three things you are forbidden from doing, taken from the repository
   rather than from your own habits.
5. **Where the repository actually is right now.** What exists, what is a placeholder, what is empty. Look,
   do not assume. A directory that exists and is empty is a different fact from one that is full.
6. **Questions.** Anything you needed and could not find a file for. Write the question. Do not answer it.

Constraints:

- Do not edit, create or delete any file.
- Do not run the build, the gates or the loop.
- Do not summarise those documents back to me at length. I have read them. Point at them.
- Do not describe what you would build. That is the next prompt.
- If you are not certain of something, mark it uncertain rather than rounding it off.

Finish with one line: the single sentence from anywhere in this repository that you think most changes how
you will work in it, quoted, with its file.
