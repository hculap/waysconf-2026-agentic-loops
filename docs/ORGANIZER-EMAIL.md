# Email for the organisers to send

Send to everyone registered for the masterclass, ideally three or more days before
16 September. It has one job: get four free accounts created before people walk into
the room, so the first fifteen minutes are not spent on sign-up forms and password
resets on a shared wifi network.

Keep it short. The long version lives on the linked page, and that page is designed
to be the thing people actually read.

---

**Subject:** Before your masterclass on Wednesday — 15 minutes of setup

**Preheader:** Four free accounts, one link. Please do this at home.

---

Hello,

You are booked into **Build an AI that checks and fixes its own work: agentic loops
in practice** with Szymon Paluch — Wednesday 16 September, 14:55, ROOM-PM.

This one is hands-on. You will build a real website with a coding agent, let it find
and fix its own mistakes, and publish it. By the end you will have a live URL you can
send to someone.

**Please spend fifteen minutes on setup before you arrive:**

**→ https://turbine-workshop.netlify.app**

That page walks you through four free accounts and one optional install. Doing it at
home rather than in the room is the difference between building something and
watching someone else build something — thirty people creating accounts on one
conference wifi network does not go well.

A few things worth saying up front:

- **You do not need to know how to code.** The session is built for designers and
  product people. There is a section on the terminal and a section on GitHub that
  assume you have used neither.
- **You do not need to install anything** if you would rather not. There is a
  browser-based option that works on any laptop, including a locked-down work one.
- **One paid AI subscription is required** — either Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus, the
  cheapest tier of either. If you already have one, you are set. If you have neither,
  the page explains what to do.
- **Bring your laptop and your charger.**

If anything on the setup page does not work for you, reply to this email before
Tuesday evening and we will sort it out then rather than at 14:55.

See you Wednesday.

---

## Notes for the organisers, not for the email

- The link above must be live before this goes out. It is deployed from
  `guideline/` in the workshop repository.
- If you can tell us the room layout — tables or theatre seating — before the day,
  it changes how the hands-on sections are run. Theatre seating with laptops on
  knees works, but the exercises get shortened.
- **One or two volunteers in the room would make a material difference.** Thirty
  people, mixed technical backgrounds, and one facilitator means someone with a
  broken setup waits. A volunteer does not need to know the material; they need to
  be able to say "paste the next prompt and carry on". A one-page
  crib sheet is in the repository at `docs/HELPER-CRIB-SHEET.md`.
- Power at seats, if it exists, is worth mentioning in your logistics email.
- The session needs a projector at 1920×1080 or better; a lot of the time is spent
  reading a terminal and a gate report on screen, and 1280×720 makes that painful.
